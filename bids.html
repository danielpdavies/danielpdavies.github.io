In preparation

QAA Collaborative Enhancement Project 2026–27

Assess-Ready: lecturer-designed AI tools and AI-aware assessment for Level 4 international business students. A cross-institution toolkit.

Theme: Future-proofing assessment. Led via Global Banking School; deadline 2 November 2026. The project would co-design, pilot and openly share an assessment redesign toolkit for Level 4 business modules, a lecturer-configured AI study-support pattern that does not produce assessable work, and staff and student guidance on transparent AI use.

Emotional intelligence and trust in generative AI
