About

I am a Business and Management lecturer with experience in module leadership, curriculum design and assessment. I have taught business in higher education since 2024, building on secondary and sixth-form teaching, including supply work, from September 2020 to March 2024.

Profile

I teach Business and Project Management at GEDU Global Education (Global Banking School, Manchester). As an Associate Lecturer in the School of Education at Liverpool John Moores University (LJMU), I also work with Level 6 undergraduates on dissertation planning, reflective writing and using theory to make sense of their experiences. I am a part-time PhD candidate in Education at LJMU.

At Study Group (LJMU International Study Centre), I led three modules for international students preparing to progress to LJMU: International Business and Environment at Level 3, and Marketing and Business Management and Business Decisions and Strategy at Level 4. I redesigned all three modules in Canvas, wrote exams and case-based assessments, and led a teaching team of three colleagues who taught on them. I was also a Teaching and Learning Champion and contributed to Study Group’s Quality and Board of Study committees.

My approach to teaching grew out of long-term supply roles teaching Business and IT in Liverpool secondary schools and sixth forms. I like my classes to be interactive and enjoyable, and I often start with a decision students can debate. In Project Management, for example, I ask students how they would get a delayed project back on schedule, what their preferred option would cost and why they would choose it. Talking it through lets them test their reasoning before they put it into writing.

My doctoral research examines formative feedback, inclusion and the responsible use of emerging technologies in higher education. It grows out of my own teaching: when students sit down to revise a piece of work, what helps them turn feedback into a stronger next draft? I hold a PGCE with QTS and a PGCHE (overall mark 70%+), and am a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy.
