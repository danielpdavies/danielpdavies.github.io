About

I have taught business in higher education since 2024, mostly with international students, building on four years in secondary and sixth-form teaching.

Profile

I am Lecturer in Business and Entrepreneurship at Global Banking School, Manchester, where I teach Level 4 Project Management, and a former module leader at Study Group (LJMU International Study Centre), where I taught Marketing and Business Strategy at Level 4 and International Business at Level 3 to 140 international students, most working in English as an additional language. I led modules across Levels 3–5.

I hold a PGCE with QTS and a PGCHE with Distinction, and am a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy. I am a part-time PhD candidate in Education at Liverpool John Moores University (in progress), researching a lecturer-fed, module-specific AI feedback agent for Level 4 international business students through practitioner action research.
