About

I have taught business in higher education since 2024, mostly with international students, building on four years in secondary and sixth-form teaching.

Profile

I am Lecturer in Business and Entrepreneurship at Global Banking School, Manchester, where I teach across Levels 3 to 7 to UK home mature students, including Level 4 Project Management and Business and Entrepreneurship. I am also a part-time PhD candidate in Education at Liverpool John Moores University (LJMU), where my doctoral research is in progress. From May 2024 to August 2026 I was Business Lecturer and Module Leader at Study Group (LJMU International Study Centre), teaching 140 international students across Levels 3 and 4, most of whom were working in English as an additional language.

I hold a PGCE with QTS and a PGCHE (overall mark 70%+), and am a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy. My doctoral research develops a lecturer-fed, module-specific AI feedback agent for Level 4 international business students and evaluates it through practitioner action research.
