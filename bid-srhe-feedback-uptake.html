In preparation

What happens after the comment?

SRHE Newer Researcher Award · Next call expected December 2026

The question

Feedback only matters if students do something with it. Generative AI now sits on both sides of that exchange: it may be permitted in the task, staff may use it to help draft comments, and students can paste a tutor’s comment into a chatbot and ask what it means. We know a good deal about how students engage with feedback in general, and much less about what happens once AI is in the loop. This one-year qualitative study looks at international Level 4 business students and is separate from my doctoral research.

Research questions

  1. How do international Level 4 business students describe what they do with written feedback on an assessment where generative AI was permitted or used?
  2. When students use AI to interpret tutor feedback, for example to translate, simplify or turn it into a plan, what changes in the meaning they take from it?
  3. Does knowing or believing that feedback was AI-assisted affect how far students trust it and act on it?

Approach

A short anonymous questionnaire gives context, followed by 12 to 16 stimulated-recall interviews in which students talk through a piece of feedback they choose to share. The analysis draws on student feedback literacy and on research into appropriate reliance on automated systems, using reflexive thematic analysis.

Planned outputs

  • A final report for SRHE and an article for SRHE News
  • A conference paper and a journal article
  • A one-page guide for module teams on writing feedback that keeps its meaning when a student runs it through a translator or chatbot

Key references

  • Carless, D. and Boud, D. (2018) ‘The development of student feedback literacy: enabling uptake of feedback’, Assessment & Evaluation in Higher Education, 43(8), pp. 1315–1325. doi:10.1080/02602938.2018.1463354
  • Lee, J.D. and See, K.A. (2004) ‘Trust in automation: designing for appropriate reliance’, Human Factors, 46(1), pp. 50–80. doi:10.1518/hfes.46.1.50_30392
