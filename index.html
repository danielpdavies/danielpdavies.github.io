Introduction

I am Lecturer in Business and Entrepreneurship at Global Banking School, Manchester, and a part-time PhD candidate in Education at Liverpool John Moores University (LJMU), where my doctoral research is in progress. From 9 November 2026 I will also be an Associate Lecturer in the School of Education at LJMU, marking Level 6 dissertations and supervising students one to one. From May 2024 to August 2026 I was Business Lecturer and Module Leader at Study Group (LJMU International Study Centre), teaching 140 international students across Levels 3 and 4, most of whom were working in English as an additional language.

My teaching and scholarship centre on assessment, feedback and learning design after generative AI, and on helping first-year international students learn to read and use academic standards. My doctoral project develops and evaluates a lecturer-fed, module-specific AI feedback agent for Level 4 international business students, through practitioner action research in the modules I teach.

I am a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy and hold a PGCHE with Distinction.
