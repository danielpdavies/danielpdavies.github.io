Introduction

I am a Business and Management lecturer with experience in module leadership, curriculum design and assessment. I am Lecturer in Business and Entrepreneurship at GEDU Global Education (Global Banking School, Manchester), where I teach Business and Project Management to UK home mature students across Levels 3 to 7. Since October 2026, I have also been an Associate Lecturer in the School of Education at Liverpool John Moores University (LJMU), where I teach and support Level 6 undergraduates in workshops and one-to-one tutorials, helping them plan their dissertations, interpret assessment briefs and develop their arguments. On the Education, Culture and Identity module, I support students with narrated presentations and reflective essays.

From March 2024 to September 2026 I was Business Lecturer and Module Leader at Study Group (LJMU International Study Centre), teaching about 140 international students preparing to progress to LJMU, most of them working in English as an additional language. In 2025/26 I led three modules across Levels 3 and 4, redesigned all three in Canvas, wrote the exams and case-based assessments, and led a teaching team of three colleagues who taught on them. I was also a Teaching and Learning Champion and contributed to Study Group’s Quality and Board of Study committees.

My approach grew out of long-term supply roles teaching Business and IT in Liverpool secondary schools and sixth forms. I like classes to be interactive, and I often start with a decision students can debate, so they can test their reasoning before they put it into writing.

My teaching and scholarship centre on assessment, feedback and learning design after generative AI, and on helping Level 4 international students learn to read and use academic standards. I am also a part-time PhD candidate in Education at LJMU. My doctoral research examines how emerging technologies can support effective feedback, academic judgement and inclusive learning in higher education.

I am a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy, hold a PGCHE (overall mark 70%+) and a PGCE with QTS, and am a Certified Digital Marketing Professional with the Digital Marketing Institute.
