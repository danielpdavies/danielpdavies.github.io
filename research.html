Research

Assessment, feedback, inclusive higher education and the responsible use of emerging technologies.

Doctoral research

I am a part-time PhD researcher in Education at Liverpool John Moores University. My research considers how generative artificial intelligence may support meaningful formative feedback and student learning in higher education.

The work brings together scholarship on assessment, feedback literacy, evaluative judgement and student agency, with particular interest in inclusive experiences for students working across different educational and language contexts.

My doctoral programme approval was ratified in September 2026. Research and development are ongoing. Detailed intervention designs, instruments, evaluation protocols and technical materials are not being shared publicly at this stage; student-related research will be undertaken only with appropriate permissions and ethical approval.

Research interests

Formative assessment and feedback; feedback literacy; evaluative judgement; curriculum and learning design; generative AI in education; inclusion and international student experience; academic integrity and responsible educational technology.

Scholarly work

A conceptual working paper, Contextualising Generative AI Feedback in Higher Education: Implications for Feedback Literacy and Student Agency (Davies, 2026), is available on Zenodo. doi:10.5281/zenodo.24295450. It is not peer reviewed.

Further manuscripts are in development. Proposals and abstracts under consideration should not be interpreted as accepted work. See Outputs for the current publication record, or Learning design for examples of my digital teaching resources.
