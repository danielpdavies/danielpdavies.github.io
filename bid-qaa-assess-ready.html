Assess-Ready: AI-aware assessment design for Level 4 business modules

QAA Collaborative Enhancement Project 2026–27 · Theme: Future-proofing assessment · Led via Global Banking School

Why this project

Most Level 4 business students now use generative AI when they prepare assessed work, yet the guidance they receive is often generic and sits in policies they rarely open. Level 4 is where study habits form. A chatbot can produce a passable risk register for a small event in seconds; what it cannot do is explain why one risk matters more than another for this event and this budget. That reasoning is what a brief should ask for and what marking guidance should reward.

Aims

  1. Co-produce assessment redesign guidance for Level 4 business modules, mapped to the AI Assessment Scale.
  2. Test AI-use statements with students to check they are understood as intended.
  3. Describe a lecturer-configured AI study-support pattern that keeps academic judgement with staff and is transparent to students.
  4. Build staff and student confidence about appropriate AI use in assessment, and share the results openly across the QAA membership.

Approach

Business lecturers and paid student partners from participating providers audit a sample of staff-authored Level 4 briefs, redesign them, and draft AI-use statements. Students test the statements against short “is this allowed?” scenarios before each partner pilots at least one redesigned brief. Evaluation uses anonymous staff and student surveys and focus groups. No marks, submitted work or VLE data are collected.

Planned outputs

  • An open toolkit for redesigning Level 4 business assessments
  • Adaptable AI-use statements, each linked to a change in task design
  • A design guide for lecturer-configured AI study support
  • Partner case studies, a workshop pack and a short sector report

Key references
